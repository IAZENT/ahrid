## Summary of Key Themes

### From Traditional vs ML Security (Ch 15)
- Traditional systems are reactive; ML enables proactive, adaptive detection
- Key gap: most ML security systems focus on network threats, not human behaviour

### From Behavioural Economics (Ch 16)
- Cognitive biases are the root cause of social engineering success
- Adaptive, personalized training can counter these biases
- Key gap: few platforms combine behavioural science with ML risk scoring

## Research Gap

> No existing platform combines:
> 1. Adaptive scenario-based awareness training
> 2. ML-driven per-user risk scoring (Random Forest)
> 3. Behavioural clustering (K-Means)
> 4. Per-prediction explainability (SHAP)
> 5. Live OSINT threat feed integration
> 6. Accessibility for non-technical SME staff in developing regions

**This gap is what AHRID addresses.**
