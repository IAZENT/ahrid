# Ethical Considerations

> Maps to **Objective 5** (Feedback & Submit)

---

## Key Ethical Areas

### 1. Surveillance vs Training
- Risk scoring could feel like employee monitoring
- How does AHRID differentiate training from surveillance?
- Informed consent requirements

### 2. Data Privacy
- What user data is collected? (attempt responses, timing, scores)
- How is it stored and protected?
- Who has access to individual risk scores?
- GDPR considerations (even if Nepal doesn't enforce GDPR, it's best practice)

### 3. Transparency & Explainability
- SHAP explanations as a transparency mechanism
- Users should understand *why* they received a risk score
- Right to explanation (GDPR Article 22)

### 4. Algorithmic Bias
- Could the RF model be biased against certain user groups?
- Synthetic data generation - does it introduce bias?
- SMOTE implications for fairness

### 5. Informed Consent
- Users should know they're being assessed
- Opt-in vs mandatory participation
- Clear communication of data usage

### 6. Governance Framework
- Who reviews the risk scores?
- Appeal mechanisms for employees
- Regular model auditing recommendations
