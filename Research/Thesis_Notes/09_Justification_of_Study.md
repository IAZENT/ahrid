# Justification of Study

> Why does this research need to exist? What gap does it fill?

---

## Justification Points

### The Problem is Real
- 85%+ of breaches involve human error (Verizon DBIR)
- SMEs are disproportionately targeted but lack resources for training
- Nepal's rapid digitization outpaces cybersecurity preparedness

### Existing Solutions Fall Short
- Enterprise platforms (CybSafe, KnowBe4) are expensive and not localized
- Generic training doesn't adapt to individual risk profiles
- No platform combines ML scoring + OSINT + explainability for SMEs

### Research Gap
- Limited research on adaptive security training for developing-country SMEs
- Few studies integrate live threat intelligence with user-specific risk models
- Ethical implications of behavioural risk scoring in SMEs are under-explored

### Why Now?
- OSINT feeds are freely available and maturing
- ML tools (scikit-learn, SHAP) are accessible to smaller development teams
- Growing regulatory interest in AI transparency and explainability
