# 📘 AHRID Thesis Outline

> **Title:** Adaptive Human Risk Intelligence Dashboard
> **Degree:** BSc (Hons) - Final Year
> **Context:** Non-technical SME staff, Kathmandu Valley

---

## Research Objectives

| # | Objective | Type | Maps To |
|---|-----------|------|---------|
| 1 | Analyse the cybersecurity threat landscape for non-technical SME staff in Kathmandu Valley - social engineering threats, cognitive vulnerabilities, and structural barriers preventing SMEs from accessing effective security awareness training | Learn & Understand | Literature Review |
| 2 | Investigate publicly accessible threat intelligence sources (Phishing.Database and AlienVault OTX) and evaluate suitability for automated scenario generation in an SME-focused training platform | Learn & Understand | Literature Review |
| 3 | Examine existing human-risk management platforms (CybSafe, KnowBe4, Hoxhunt) - identifying gaps in live threat integration, per-user explainability, and accessibility for non-technical SME staff in developing-country contexts | Learn & Understand | Case Studies |
| 4 | Design, develop, and evaluate AHRID by integrating live phishing URL ingestion with Random Forest, K-Means, and SHAP - assessing performance through F1-score, PR-AUC, and Cohen's Kappa against a rule-based baseline | Design & Develop | RQ1 · H1 |
| 5 | Investigate the ethical implications of deploying automated behavioural risk scoring in SME environments - surveillance boundaries, transparency mechanisms, and governance requirements | Feedback & Submit | RQ2 · H2 |
