# Contribution & Significance

> What does this thesis add to the field?

---

## Key Contributions

1. **Novel platform integration** - First platform to combine adaptive scenario training + RF risk scoring + K-Means clustering + SHAP explainability + live OSINT feeds in a single system
2. **SME accessibility** - Designed for non-technical staff in developing-country SMEs (not enterprise-only)
3. **Per-user explainability** - SHAP-based individual risk explanations (most platforms give aggregate scores only)
4. **Live threat integration** - Real-time OSINT feeds driving scenario generation (not static question banks)
5. **Ethical framework** - Explicit examination of surveillance vs training boundaries in automated risk scoring
6. **Nepal context** - One of few studies examining cybersecurity awareness for Kathmandu Valley SMEs

## Significance
- Addresses the "awareness training gap" for SMEs that can't afford enterprise solutions
- Provides a replicable, open approach to adaptive security training
- Contributes ethical guidelines for deploying behavioural monitoring in workplaces
