> Maps to **Objective 5** - How ethics shaped the development process

## Ethics-by-Design Decisions

### Data Minimisation
- Only collect data necessary for risk scoring
- No keystroke logging, screen recording, or browsing history

### Transparency Mechanisms Built In
- SHAP explanations shown to users (not just admins)
- Risk scores are explainable, not opaque numbers
- Users can see which factors contribute to their score

### Consent & Communication
- Clear onboarding explaining what data is collected
- Training framed as development, not surveillance

### Bias Mitigation
- SMOTE used to prevent model bias toward majority class
- Synthetic data generation across diverse risk profiles
- Regular model evaluation with fairness metrics
