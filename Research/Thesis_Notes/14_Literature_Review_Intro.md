## Purpose
- Set the scope of the literature review
- Describe the search strategy (databases, keywords, inclusion/exclusion criteria)
- Outline the structure of the review

## Search Strategy

### Databases Used
- Google Scholar
- IEEE Xplore
- ACM Digital Library
- Semantic Scholar
- arXiv

### Search Terms
See [[00_LIT_REVIEW_TRACKER]] for full list of search queries.

### Inclusion Criteria
- Published 2018-2026 (recent 8 years)
- Peer-reviewed journals or reputable conferences
- Written in English
- Relevant to: cybersecurity awareness, ML in security, OSINT, XAI, or human risk

### Exclusion Criteria
- Purely theoretical with no empirical evaluation
- Focused solely on network/infrastructure security (not human factor)
- Non-English publications

## Review Structure Overview
1. Traditional vs ML-based security systems
2. Behavioural economics and human risk
3. Case studies of existing platforms
4. Synthesis and gap identification
