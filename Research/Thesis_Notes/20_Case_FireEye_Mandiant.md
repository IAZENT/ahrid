# Case Study: FireEye Mandiant

> Maps to **Objective 3** - Examine existing platforms

---

## Overview
- **Company:** FireEye / Mandiant (now part of Google Cloud)
- **Product:** Mandiant Threat Intelligence + Security Validation
- **Type:** Threat intel + incident response
- **Target:** Enterprise / Government

## Key Features
- World-class incident response team
- Threat intelligence from frontline investigations
- Attack surface management
- Security validation (breach simulation)

## Gaps (Relevant to AHRID)
- Consulting/service model - not a self-serve training platform
- No adaptive per-user training
- No ML-based individual risk scoring
- Cost prohibitive for SMEs
