# Research Questions

---

## RQ1 (Technical) - Maps to Objective 4, Hypothesis H1

> **To what extent can a platform integrating live OSINT threat feeds with Random Forest classification, K-Means behavioural clustering, and SHAP explainability improve cybersecurity risk prediction accuracy compared to a rule-based scoring baseline?**

### Sub-questions
- What level of classification accuracy (F1, PR-AUC, Cohen's Kappa) does the RF model achieve?
- How effectively does K-Means segment users into meaningful behavioural risk profiles?
- Does SHAP provide actionable per-user risk explanations?

---

## RQ2 (Ethical) - Maps to Objective 5, Hypothesis H2

> **What are the ethical implications of deploying automated behavioural risk scoring in SME environments, particularly regarding employee surveillance, transparency, and governance?**

### Sub-questions
- Where is the boundary between training and surveillance?
- What transparency mechanisms are needed for ethical deployment?
- What governance frameworks should guide automated risk scoring?
