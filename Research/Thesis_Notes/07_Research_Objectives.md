# Research Objectives

---

| # | Objective | Type | Maps To |
|---|-----------|------|---------|
| 1 | To analyse the cybersecurity threat landscape for non-technical SME staff in Kathmandu Valley, focusing on social engineering threats, human cognitive vulnerabilities, and the structural barriers that prevent SMEs from accessing effective security awareness training. | Learn & Understand | Literature Review |
| 2 | To investigate publicly accessible threat intelligence sources - Phishing.Database and AlienVault OTX - and evaluate their suitability for automated scenario generation in an SME-focused training platform, with honest assessment of the intelligence depth each source provides. | Learn & Understand | Literature Review |
| 3 | To examine existing human-risk management platforms - CybSafe, KnowBe4, and Hoxhunt - identifying gaps in live threat integration, per-user explainability, and accessibility for non-technical SME staff in developing-country contexts. | Learn & Understand | Case Studies |
| 4 | To design, develop, and evaluate AHRID by integrating live phishing URL ingestion with Random Forest classification, K-Means clustering, and SHAP explainability, assessing technical performance through F1-score, PR-AUC, and Cohen's Kappa against a rule-based scoring baseline. | Design & Develop | RQ1 · H1 |
| 5 | To investigate the ethical implications of deploying automated behavioural risk scoring in SME environments - examining surveillance boundaries, transparency mechanisms, and governance requirements - and document findings, limitations, and recommendations in this thesis. | Feedback & Submit | RQ2 · H2 |

## Notes
- Objectives 1-3 are "Learn & Understand" - addressed through literature review and case studies
- Objective 4 is "Design & Develop" - the core technical contribution
- Objective 5 is "Feedback & Submit" - ethical reflection throughout
