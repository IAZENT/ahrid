# Problem Context

> Maps to **Objective 1**: Analyse the cybersecurity threat landscape for non-technical SME staff in Kathmandu Valley.

---

## Key Points to Cover
- Kathmandu Valley's digital transformation (banking, fintech, SMEs going online)
- Social engineering threats: phishing, vishing, smishing, USB baiting, etc.
- Human cognitive vulnerabilities: authority bias, urgency, curiosity
- Structural barriers: budget, expertise, awareness gaps in Nepali SMEs
- The "awareness training gap" - enterprise tools exist but SMEs can't access them
- Nepal-specific: limited local cybersecurity research, reliance on global solutions

## Research & Data Sources
- Nepal Rastra Bank cybersecurity guidelines
- Verizon Data Breach Investigations Report (DBIR)
- NIST Cybersecurity Framework
- Local news on cyber incidents in Nepal
