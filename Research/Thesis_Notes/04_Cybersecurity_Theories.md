# Cybersecurity Theories

> Theoretical frameworks that underpin AHRID's approach to human-risk management.

---

## Theories to Cover

### Protection Motivation Theory (PMT)
- Rogers (1975, 1983)
- Threat appraisal + coping appraisal → protection motivation
- How AHRID leverages PMT: risk scores as threat appraisal, training as coping

### Theory of Planned Behaviour (TPB)
- Ajzen (1991)
- Attitudes, subjective norms, perceived control → behavioural intention
- Relevance: why people ignore security warnings

### Human Factors in Security
- Reason's Swiss Cheese Model
- The "weakest link" paradigm
- Security fatigue (Stanton et al.)

### Behavioural Nudge Theory
- Thaler & Sunstein (2008)
- How AHRID nudges: adaptive difficulty, SHAP explanations as nudges

### Situated Learning Theory
- Lave & Wenger (1991)
- Learning through realistic scenarios (AHRID's scenario engine)
