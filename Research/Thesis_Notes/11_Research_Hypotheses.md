## H1 (Technical) - Maps to RQ1, Objective 4

> **AHRID's ML pipeline (Random Forest + SMOTE + 14-feature vector) will achieve a statistically significant improvement in risk prediction over a rule-based scoring baseline, as measured by F1-score gap ≥ 15 points.**

### Current Evidence
- RF accuracy: 91.4%
- F1: 0.89
- PR-AUC: 0.92
- Cohen's Kappa: 0.875
- F1 gap over baseline: **52.2 points** (target was 15)
- ✅ H1 strongly supported
