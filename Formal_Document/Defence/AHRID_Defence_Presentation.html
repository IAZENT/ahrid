06 — ML Pipeline

SHAP: Every Prediction Explained

How TreeSHAP Works

  • Game-theoretic feature attribution
  • Exact Shapley values for tree ensembles
  • O(TLD²) — polynomial, not exponential
  • Properties: efficiency, symmetry, consistency
  • Superior to LIME (unstable, approximate)

08 — Ethics

Ethical Framework & IPA 2075 Compliance

Privacy-by-Design Principles

  • Data minimisation — 18 behavioural features, zero PII
  • Purpose limitation — risk assessment & training only
  • Synthetic data — no real employee records processed
  • Right to explanation — SHAP per prediction
  • CASCADE deletion — user removal = full data wipe
  • RBAC enforcement — role-based access at API level

Non-Punitive Governance

High risk ≠ Disciplinary action

Elevated risk triggers:
✓ Additional training scenarios
✓ Personalised learning path
✓ SHAP-guided improvement tips

✗ No performance reviews
✗ No managerial warnings
✗ No employment consequences
AI4People (Floridi et al., 2018):
Beneficence · Non-maleficence · Autonomy · Justice · Explicability

10 — Limitations

Honest Limitations

Data & Validation

  • Synthetic data — validates architecture, not operational performance
  • No real-user deployment or testing
  • No longitudinal behavioural study
  • Single classifier comparison (RF vs baseline only)

Scope & Access

  • Kathmandu Valley SMEs only
  • English-only (no Nepali localisation)
  • Moderate silhouette coefficient
  • No adversarial robustness testing
Each limitation is explicitly acknowledged in the thesis and paired with a future work recommendation
