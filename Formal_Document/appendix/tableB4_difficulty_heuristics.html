Evaluation follows strict priority: Level 1 signals checked first, then Level 3 signals, with Level 2 as the default fallback. This ordering ensures that a URL triggering both a free TLD (Level 1) and cloud hosting (Level 3) is conservatively classified at the lower difficulty.
