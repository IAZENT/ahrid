Figure B2. Threat Classification Workflow

How a raw phishing URL becomes a categorised, difficulty-rated training scenario

1
Raw URL Input
Ingested from Phishing.Database or AlienVault OTX feed
▼
2
Keyword Detection
Domain keywords matched against brand dictionary
Path tokens scanned for lure signals (e.g. "invoice", "reset", "prize")
▼
3
Lure Type Assignment
One of 6 lure types assigned based on keyword pattern match
credential_harvest invoice_fraud delivery_notification it_support ceo_impersonation prize_scam
▼
4
Category Mapping
Lure type maps to 1–3 scenario categories · SHA-1(URL) mod n selects final category
phishing_email smishing vishing password_hygiene social_engineering usb_baiting data_handling physical_security
▼
5
Difficulty Classification
URL structure analysed: scheme · TLD · subdomain depth · IDN encoding · cloud hosting
Level 1 — Obvious (10 XP) Level 2 — Subtle (15 XP) Level 3 — Advanced (25 XP)
▼
6
Training Scenario Created
Scenario record written with: category · difficulty · lure_type · target_roles · MCQ options · red_flags · explanation · learning_tip · source=osint

The SHA-1 deterministic routing at Step 4 ensures reproducibility: the same URL always maps to the same category, preventing duplicates and guaranteeing balanced scenario pool coverage across all 8 categories.
