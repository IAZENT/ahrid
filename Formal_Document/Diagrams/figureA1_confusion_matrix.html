Figure A1. Confusion matrix for the Random Forest classifier on the held-out test set (n=210). Rows = actual class; columns = predicted class. Diagonal cells (correct predictions) are shaded green; off-diagonal cells (misclassifications) are shaded red proportional to count. Zero false positives for Low Risk confirms no safe employee was escalated. The dominant off-diagonal pattern is adjacent-class confusion (e.g., High → Medium), consistent with smooth behavioural risk gradients rather than gross misclassification. Overall accuracy = 191/210 = 90.95%.
