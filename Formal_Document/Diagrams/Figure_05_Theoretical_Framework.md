### Detailed design instructions

**Central AHRID circle:**
- Large circle, diameter ~180px
- Background: #1B2A4A (dark navy)
- Text: "AHRID" in white Montserrat Bold 22pt
- Sub-text: "Adaptive Human-Risk Intelligence Dashboard" in white 10pt
- Three arrows radiating outward to the three theory boxes (or inward from theory boxes to AHRID)

**Three theory boxes (top row):**
Each box: rounded rectangle, 280 x 120px

| Theory | Background | Border |
|--------|-----------|--------|
| PMT | #EBF5FB (light blue) | #2980B9 |
| Prospect Theory | #FEF9E7 (light yellow) | #F39C12 |
| TAM | #EAFAF1 (light green) | #27AE60 |

Text in each theory box:
- Theory name: Montserrat Bold 15pt, dark #2C3E50
- Author + year: Open Sans 11pt, grey #777777

**Middle row — Core construct boxes:**
Same background as parent theory box, slightly lighter.
Text: Open Sans Regular 13pt.
Content:
- PMT: "Threat appraisal activates coping motivation; non-technical employees need risk explained in human terms"
- Prospect Theory: "Loss aversion + availability heuristic cause employees to underestimate everyday threat probability"
- TAM: "Perceived usefulness and ease of use determine adoption; complexity triggers avoidance"

**Bottom row — AHRID Design Response boxes:**
Background: white. Bold green top border 3px #27AE60.
Text: Open Sans Regular 13pt.
Label header: "AHRID Design Response" in bold #27AE60, 11pt.
Content:
- PMT response: "Risk scores paired with specific actionable guidance; non-punitive framing preserves coping motivation"
- Prospect Theory response: "Live Phishing.Database + AlienVault OTX feeds; Nepal-contextualised scenarios (eSewa, Ncell)"
- TAM response: "SHAP plain-language attributions; scenario completion target under 5 minutes; mobile-first layout"

**Connecting arrows:**
- Theory box to construct box: solid downward arrow, colour matching theory
- Construct box to AHRID response: solid downward arrow, same colour
- Theory box to AHRID centre: dashed arrow, grey #AAAAAA

**Footer:**
"Three cognitive biases addressed: loss aversion, availability heuristic, confirmation bias (Kahneman and Tversky, 1979; Workman, 2008)"
Open Sans Italic 11pt, grey #777777
