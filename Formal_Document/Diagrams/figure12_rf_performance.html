Figure 12. Random Forest classifier per-class performance on the held-out test set (n=210, 80:20 stratified split). Bars show Precision (P), Recall (R), and F1-score for each of the four risk tiers. Low Risk (Class 0) achieves perfect precision (1.000) — zero safe employees misclassified as at-risk. Critical Risk (Class 3) achieves highest recall (0.943) — 94.3% of the most dangerous profiles correctly identified. Macro-averaged F1=0.889; overall accuracy=90.95%; Cohen’s κ=0.868 (“almost perfect”). Baseline rule-based classifier F1=0.387 (+50.21 percentage-point improvement).
